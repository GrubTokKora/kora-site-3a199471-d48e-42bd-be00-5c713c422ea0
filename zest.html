How ZEST

works.

Everyone has microscopic Demodex mites in their eyelash follicles. When they multiply, together with the biofilm and debris they leave behind, the lids become itchy and inflamed and the oil glands suffer.

ZEST uses a cleansing gel that breaks down that biofilm and clears the mites and debris from the base of the lashes, leaving the lids clean and the eyes looking brighter.

Who it helps

Is ZEST right for you?

We recommend it after an evaluation, often alongside other treatments. It is typically considered for:

  • Demodex blepharitis and itchy lash lines
  • Crusting or flakes at the base of the lashes
  • Dry eye made worse by debris and biofilm
  • Patients who want a quick, no-downtime treatment

What a session is like

The gel is worked gently along the lash line and then removed, taking the biofilm and debris with it. It fits easily into a lunch hour.

ZEST is often the first step before light-based therapy, so that the glands are clean when OptiLIGHT or OptiPLUS gets to work.

Questions

No. You can go straight back to work or your day.

Does ZEST treat Demodex mites?

Yes. It is designed to remove the mites, biofilm and debris that gather around the lashes.

Yes. ZEST is safe and comfortable for patients of all ages.
