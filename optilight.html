How OptiLIGHT

works.

OptiLIGHT is a light-based treatment for dry eye. Precise pulses of light and gentle warmth are applied to the skin below the eyes, where they settle the inflammation and the tiny abnormal blood vessels that keep the eyelid oil glands from working.

When those glands work again, the oil layer of your tears returns and your tears stop evaporating so quickly. That is why OptiLIGHT treats the cause of evaporative dry eye rather than the dryness you feel on the surface.

Who it helps

Is OptiLIGHT right for you?

We recommend it after an evaluation, often alongside other treatments. It is typically considered for:

  • Dry eye caused by meibomian gland dysfunction
  • Ocular rosacea and red, inflamed eyelid margins
  • Watery eyes that are really a sign of poor tear quality
  • Patients who have noticed gland loss or thickened oil
  • Recurrent styes linked to blocked glands

What a session is like

You lie back comfortably with protective shields over your eyes. A cooling gel goes on the skin, and the light is applied in a series of quick, bright pulses. Most patients feel warmth and a light snap.

Dr. Lazar often follows OptiLIGHT with gentle gland expression, and sometimes pairs it with low-level light therapy for a stronger, longer-lasting effect. Treatment is usually a short series of visits; we confirm the number at your evaluation.

Questions

OptiLIGHT, answered

Does OptiLIGHT hurt?

It is non-invasive and most patients describe it as a warm, quick snap on the skin. Your eyes stay protected by shields throughout.

Is OptiLIGHT right for every skin tone?

Light-based treatments depend on skin type. We check your suitability at the evaluation, and if OptiLIGHT is not the best fit, options such as TearCare or low-level light therapy may be.

How soon will I notice a difference?

Many patients notice their eyes feel more comfortable as they move through the series. Because the treatment restores gland function, the improvement builds over the course of the plan.
