How OptiPLUS

works.

OptiPLUS uses two radiofrequency settings, 1 MHz and 4 MHz, to warm different depths of tissue around the eyes. That gentle heat improves circulation and melts the thickened oil sitting inside the meibomian glands, so the glands can release it again.

It is designed for the evaporative type of dry eye, where tears are present but break down too quickly. A welcome side effect is a firmer, smoother look to the skin of the lids, because the same warmth encourages collagen.

Who it helps

Is OptiPLUS right for you?

We recommend it after an evaluation, often alongside other treatments. It is typically considered for:

  • Evaporative dry eye and meibomian gland dysfunction
  • Thick, stagnant oil that will not clear with warm compresses
  • Styes and blocked glands that keep coming back
  • Patients who also want firmer eyelid skin

What a session is like

A smooth handpiece is moved slowly over the skin around the eyes while you relax. It feels like a warm massage, and you can return to your day straight afterwards.

Dr. Lazar frequently combines OptiPLUS with OptiLIGHT. One addresses inflammation, the other the oil itself, and together they tackle both halves of gland dysfunction.

Questions

OptiPLUS, answered

What does OptiPLUS feel like?

Most patients describe it as a warm, relaxing massage around the eyes. There is no downtime.

Is OptiPLUS the same as OptiLIGHT?

No. OptiLIGHT uses pulses of light to calm inflammation; OptiPLUS uses radiofrequency warmth to free up the oil in the glands. They are often used together.

Can OptiPLUS help with styes?

Yes. Because it melts and mobilises trapped oil, it is part of our same-day stye treatment and helps prevent new ones.
