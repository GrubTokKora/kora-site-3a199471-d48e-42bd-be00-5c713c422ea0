The approach

A proper look first. Then a plan that leans less on drops.

DRY EYECARE is the dry eye practice of Dr. Inna Lazar, founder of PRO EYECARE. It exists for one condition, and it starts with you rather than with a bottle.

If you have been reaching for drops every day and your eyes still feel gritty or tired, you are probably here because that routine has run its course.

So the first visit is a dedicated dry eye evaluation. Its job is to understand what is actually happening with your eyes before anyone suggests a treatment.

What follows is a plan built around in-office treatments that restore the eyelid oil glands. The aim is simple: to help you rely far less on eye drops.

Treatment

The treatment, and why it works.

One in-office treatment, explained in plain terms. Whether it suits you is decided at your evaluation.

OptiLIGHT

Dry Eye Care · In-office

Intense pulsed light that calms the inflammation behind meibomian gland dysfunction, the most common driver of dry eye.

The meibomian glands are the oil glands along your eyelids. OptiLIGHT is part of a plan built around restoring them, carried out here in the office rather than at home with another bottle.
