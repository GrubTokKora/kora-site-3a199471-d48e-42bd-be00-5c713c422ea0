About DRY EYECARE

Dry eye care led by Dr. Inna Lazar.

A dedicated dry eye practice inside PRO EYECARE, built by an optometrist who teaches other doctors how to treat it, with offices in Greenwich and Darien, Connecticut.

Why a dry eye

practice of its own?

Dr. Lazar founded PRO EYECARE alongside a specialty practice for drop-less dry eye treatment, because she kept meeting patients who had spent years cycling through drops that never fixed the problem.

DRY EYECARE is that specialty work, given a home of its own. It is the same doctors, the same offices and the same phone number as PRO EYECARE, focused entirely on finding the cause of dry eye and treating it with FDA-approved and FDA-cleared technology.

Dr. Lazar also lectures to eye care professionals across the United States on dry eye management, so the care you receive reflects what is working in the field today.

How we work

Three things every patient can expect

01

The cause, not the symptom

We look at the glands, lids and tear film behind your discomfort rather than reaching straight for another prescription drop.

02

Plans built around your life

Using drops several times a day is hard to keep up. We favour in-office, drop-less therapies that fit a busy schedule.

03

Honest expectations

You will know what each treatment involves, how many visits to expect and how we will measure progress before you commit.
