Darien questions

Visiting our Darien office

When is the Darien office open late?

The Darien office is open until 6:00 pm on Thursdays. Early or late appointments on other days can be arranged by request.

Is the Darien office open on weekends?

The Darien office is closed on Saturdays and Sundays. If you need a Saturday visit, our Greenwich office offers Saturday appointments by request.

Can I split visits between Darien and Greenwich?

Yes. Many patients start in one office and continue in the other. Your records and plan are shared across both.
