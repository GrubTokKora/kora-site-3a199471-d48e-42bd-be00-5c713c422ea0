Same-day stye treatment in Greenwich & Darien, CT.

A stye is a painful, swollen bump on the eyelid caused by a blocked, infected oil gland. Rather than waiting it out, we treat it the same day, usually in under 30 minutes with Dr. Lazar.

Book an Evaluation Call (203) 202-0202

Why styes happen

Each eyelid has a row of oil glands. When one becomes blocked and bacteria take hold, it swells into a tender red bump. People with blepharitis, Demodex mites or meibomian gland dysfunction are far more prone to them, which is why styes often keep coming back.

How we treat a stye the same day

Our same-day treatment combines two technologies:

  • OptiLIGHT light-based therapy to calm inflammation and help clear the bacteria in the gland
  • OptiPLUS dual-frequency radiofrequency to warm and melt the trapped oil so the gland can drain

Stopping the next one

Because the same treatments improve how the glands work, they also help prevent new styes. If yours keep returning, we will look for the underlying cause, often blepharitis or blocked glands, and treat that too.

When to call us

Call if a stye is painful, growing, affecting your vision, or has not improved within a few days. A bump that has hardened and stopped hurting may be a chalazion, which we can also assess.

Questions

Common questions

How long does stye treatment take?

Typically less than 30 minutes with Dr. Lazar.

Should I squeeze a stye?

No. Squeezing can spread infection and inflammation. Warm compresses are fine while you wait for your appointment.

What is the difference between a stye and a chalazion?

A stye is an infected, painful gland. A chalazion is a firmer, usually painless lump from a blocked gland without active infection. We can treat both.
