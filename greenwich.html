Greenwich questions

Visiting our Greenwich office

Is the Greenwich office open on Saturdays?

Saturday appointments in Greenwich are available by request. Call (203) 202-0202 and we will arrange a time with you.

Can I come in after work?

Yes. The Greenwich office is open until 6:00 pm on Tuesdays, and early or late appointments can be arranged by request on other days.

Do I need to see Dr. Lazar in Greenwich?

No. You can be seen in Greenwich or Darien and switch between them for follow-up visits if that is easier.
